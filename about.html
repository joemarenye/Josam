About

I work at the point where structural engineering, site delivery, and numbers meet. Checking that a bar bending schedule reconciles, that a program of works is realistic, and that a spreadsheet actually balances before it goes to a client.

My background is in structural and civil engineering, with hands-on site experience and a parallel life in financial analysis and technical writing. On site, I've supervised earthworks on a demolition-and-restoration project. Dredging, backfilling, and compaction with rollers and other heavy plant. On the design and documentation side, I've prepared bar bending schedules for footbridge structures, road marking quantities and drawings for BOQ purposes, and programs of work for project scheduling.

Outside pure engineering, I build the tools that keep a small operation honest: Excel-based financial trackers with live dashboards, a vendor stock-and-sales tracking system, and increasingly, software, from e-commerce platforms to a restaurant website built from scratch. I also write, professionally, when the brief calls for it. Academic and technical writing alongside the engineering documentation.
